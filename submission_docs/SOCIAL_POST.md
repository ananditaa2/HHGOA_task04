**Excited to unveil TigerDetect: Autonomous Agentic AI Fraud Investigation & Next-Best Action Platform!** 🐅🌴

Built for the **TigerGraph × Hacker House Goa (IEEE-CIS Fraud Investigation Edition)** hackathon.

In banking, financial crime teams face noisy, incomplete, and uncertain alerts. Analysts waste hours traversing disjointed transaction logs, customer profiles, device fingerprints, and legacy cases.

**TigerDetect** fundamentally reimagines fraud intelligence with **7 Core Strategic Innovations**:

1. **Policy Rules as a Graph**: Bank fraud policies R1–R10 are modeled as traversable vertices with `OVERRIDES` and `ESCALATES_TO` edges resolved dynamically.
2. **Evidence Conflict Scoring (Rule R8)**: An explicit mathematical conflict metric ($C \in [0.0, 1.0]$) that quantifies signal discordance.
3. **Graph Centrality for Device Rings**: Local PageRank and Degree Centrality algorithms distinguishing core hub cards from peripheral cards in coordinated rings (Case HHG-014).
4. **Time-Decay Weighted Case Memory**: Exponential half-life decay ($\tau = 45$ days) prioritizing recent historical precedents.
5. **Adversarial Self-Critique Agent ("Devil's Advocate")**: Challenges fraud hypotheses to protect legitimate customers from card blocks during travel or recurring renewals.
6. **Literal Graph Provenance Paths**: Machine-readable graph traversals attached to every piece of evidence.
7. **Post-Run 20-Case Multi-Dimensional QA Audit**: 100% schema compliance, full rule coverage, and FinCEN SAR integrity across all 20 benchmark cases.
